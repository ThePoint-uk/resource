The problem

Your contracts live in spreadsheets, inboxes and memory.

Notice periods slip by. Contracts quietly auto-renew on old terms. Insurance certificates expire unnoticed. And when the auditor asks who approved a £1m deal, the answer is an email thread.

The product

Everything about every supplier and contract, in one place.

Thirteen purpose-built components, designed to feel like premium software, not a SharePoint list.

Renewal timeline

Every contract on one timeline, with notice windows shaded, deadlines marked and auto-renewals flagged. Spot the next twelve months' decisions at a glance.

Vendor 360

Contracts, spend, scorecards, risks, certificates, documents and full history for each supplier, on one screen.

Contract lifecycle

FAQ

Questions IT and finance ask us.

Something else? Ask us directly.

Do our users need extra Microsoft licences?

Keystone is designed to run on the Power Apps and Power Automate rights included with most Microsoft 365 business and enterprise plans, using standard connectors only. You'll need one licensed service account. We confirm your licensing fit on a free readiness call before you buy.

Where is our data stored?

In a SharePoint site in your own tenant, in your Microsoft data region. Keystone has no external servers and sends nothing to us. A Power Platform environment holds the app itself (not your data).

What do we need technically?

A Power Platform environment with Dataverse to hold the Keystone solution, an admin who can create a SharePoint site, and your data-loss-prevention policy allowing SharePoint, Approvals, Teams and Outlook connectors together. The installation guide includes a full checklist.

Can we change the reminder schedule and approval thresholds ourselves?

Yes. That's what the Rule Builder is for. Administrators edit, simulate and publish rules without a developer, and every version is kept and audited.

Can we import our existing contract register?

Yes, from our Excel template. Re-imports update matching records rather than creating duplicates. The Guided Installation Day includes importing up to 500 contracts.

Is it really a one-off payment?

Yes. You buy a perpetual licence for your tenant, with unlimited users, and it keeps working for as long as you use it. The first 12 months of updates and support are included. After that, renewing updates & support (£599 a year) is optional. It's recommended, because Microsoft regularly updates the Power Platform. Your data always stays in your SharePoint and can be exported at any time.

Can Keystone be customised?

Most organisations need only rules, settings, templates and branding, all of which are self-service. For anything further, our consultants can build supported extensions at our day rate.
