      How to play

      1. Flip a card instead of rolling. Move one peg that many holes clockwise.
      2. A 6 (Cheetah) lets you bring a peg out onto your start hole, and gives you another go.
      3. Land on someone and you send their peg back home. +5 points.
      4. Get a peg into your coloured finish lane for +10. You need the exact number to fit, and you can't land on your own pegs.
      5. SNAP! When a card matches the one underneath, anyone can hit their snap button. Fastest player wins the whole pile: 1 point per card. Snap at the wrong time and lose 2.
      6. First with all four pegs in the finish lane wins the round (+20). Points carry over if you play again.
